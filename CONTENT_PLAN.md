# MythicalHelper Content Plan

## Goal

Make MythicalHelper easier to discover by leading with the parent moment:

> A free, open-source certificate for the day they ask who carried the magic.

## Passive Discovery System

The project should not depend on the maintainer manually posting every week. The low-maintenance discovery loop is:

1. Search guides answer real parent questions.
2. The demo and certificate flow give visitors something concrete to inspect.
3. Verification pages include a light open-source attribution link.
4. The share page gives supporters copy they can reuse without asking for a custom pitch.
5. GitHub topics and README copy help open-source discovery.

## Weekly Rhythm

Use this only when someone wants to actively help. The project should still make sense without it.

- 2 Instagram posts
- 3 Pinterest pins
- 1 community share or comment thread
- 1 search-focused guide update or new page

## Instagram Posts

1. **Question Carousel**
   - Slide 1: "What do you say when they ask if Santa was real?"
   - Slide 2: "Start with what they noticed."
   - Slide 3: "Tell the truth without calling it a trick."
   - Slide 4: "Show them who carried the magic."
   - CTA: "View the demo certificate in bio."

2. **Certificate Reel**
   - Shot 1: Fill record
   - Shot 2: Certificate appears
   - Shot 3: QR verification page opens
   - CTA: "Free, open source, and made for parents."

3. **Open Source Trust Post**
   - Hook: "This is not a paid template shop."
   - Body: "MythicalHelper is free, nonprofit, and open source."
   - CTA: "Use it or help improve it on GitHub."

4. **Tooth Fairy Post**
   - Hook: "When they ask if the Tooth Fairy was you..."
   - Body: "You can tell the truth and still keep the milestone warm."
   - CTA: "Read the parent guide."

5. **Save It For Later**
   - Hook: "You do not need this today. You may want it someday."
   - Body: "Create the record now, reveal it when the moment arrives."
   - CTA: "Create a certificate."

## Community Sharing

Tone matters. Post as a builder and parent-friendly open-source maintainer, not as an advertiser.

Suggested short post:

> I built a free open-source project called MythicalHelper for parents who want a gentle way to handle the day a child asks who carried the magic behind Santa, the Tooth Fairy, or similar traditions. It creates a printable certificate with a QR verification page. I would love feedback from parents on whether the framing feels warm and honest.

Good places to test carefully:

- parenting subreddits where project sharing is allowed
- local parent Facebook groups that allow resources
- indie maker communities
- open-source showcases
- holiday planning communities in October and November
