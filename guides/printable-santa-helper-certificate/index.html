Printable Certificate

Printable Santa helper certificate with QR verification

MythicalHelper creates a printable certificate for parents, guardians, and caregivers who quietly carried the Santa tradition with care.

What makes it different

Most printable Santa certificates are for children. This one recognizes the trusted helper behind the work and includes a QR code that opens a public verification page.

How it works

Create a record, choose the relevant realms, preview the certificate, print the PDF, and save it until the reveal conversation feels right.

Free and open source

The project is nonprofit and open source. The goal is not to sell a template, but to give families a gentle tool they can use or improve.
