# MythicalHelper Marketing Notes

## Positioning

MythicalHelper is not a generic printable certificate template. It is a free, open-source, nonprofit project and a gentle reveal tool for parents who want childhood wonder to remain meaningful after a child starts asking who carried the magic.

Core line:

> A certificate for the day they ask who carried the magic.

## Instagram Starter Series

Use Instagram for emotional proof and visual discovery. Pair every post with a link in bio to `https://mythicalhelper.org/` and a visible demo CTA.

1. **The Question**
   - Visual: close-up of the certificate seal or QR corner.
   - Caption hook: "What do you say when they finally ask if Santa was real?"
   - CTA: "Create a keepsake for that conversation."

2. **The Reframe**
   - Visual: parent hands holding a printed certificate.
   - Caption hook: "It was never just pretending. It was care, carried quietly."
   - CTA: "See the demo record."

3. **The Product**
   - Visual: screen recording from create record to certificate.
   - Caption hook: "A printable certificate with a QR verification page."
   - CTA: "Make one before the moment arrives."

4. **The Realms**
   - Visual: four simple slides for Wonderkeeping, North Pole, Tooth Fairy, Easter.
   - Caption hook: "For every little tradition you kept alive."
   - CTA: "Choose the realms your household served."

5. **The Reveal**
   - Visual: certificate beside a holiday box, memory folder, or envelope.
   - Caption hook: "Save it for the day the story changes."
   - CTA: "Create the record now, reveal it later."

6. **The Open Source Note**
   - Visual: calm brand graphic with logo and certificate detail.
   - Caption hook: "Free, nonprofit, and open source."
   - CTA: "Use it, share it, or help improve it on GitHub."

## Channel Guidance

Instagram is a good first channel, but a few posts alone will probably not be enough. The product solves a seasonal, emotionally specific problem, so pair Instagram with:

- search pages/articles for parents asking how to explain Santa, Tooth Fairy, or Easter Bunny truth gently
- Pinterest pins that show the printable certificate and verification page
- small parenting community posts framed as a free tool, not an ad
- a holiday push starting in early November for Christmas-related searches

The best traffic pattern is: emotional social post -> homepage/demo -> certificate creation.

Keep the tone transparent and community-minded. Do not position MythicalHelper like a scarcity-driven paid template shop; the open-source status is part of the product's credibility.
